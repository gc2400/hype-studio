    Clocks
    #TypeWhichHow manyStart

    Every hour starts with the top of the hour (fixed time). A clock with "goes on where it left off" then continues with its next slot; other clocks start at slot 1 every hour (and at a show start on the half hour). When a clock is done before the hour ends it starts again. A slot plays the amount you choose (all = one full round, fill = until the hour or show ends), then the next slot follows. The top-of-the-hour sweeper is a fixed time (every hour). Never more than 2 jingles in a row, and artist separation always applies.

    On screen · show your graphics on TV when something plays

    Upload your graphics to branding/screen (FileZilla). Make them 1920×1080 with a transparent background, as PNG (or a transparent WebM for animation) – they are placed full screen, so the position is in the image itself. Example: New Music image when a song from NEW plays, 3 s after the start, for 10 s.

    Recordings · the show is recorded by itself

    Recordings go to the Library → Recordings (also music/MIXES/Recordings in WinSCP): the TV picture as .mp4 and the radio as .mp3, named date time name. Show = while that show is on air (website schedule). Day + time = your own window (an end before the start runs over midnight). A recording you start yourself (REC / GO LIVE) always wins, and if you stop an automatic one with REC it stays off until its window ends.

    Ads · sponsor spots (audio or video) · X plays per day

    Put spot files in the ads folder (one subfolder per brand, e.g. ads/Brand). Here you set how often each spot plays. They play in ad breaks: put an Ad break slot in a clock (the number = spots in that break) and/or add a fixed time Ad break (e.g. every hour at *:20, after the song). A break gets the spots that are most behind on their plays for today, max. 1 per brand. Video spots show full screen on TV; audio spots play over the Hype Screen.

    Fixed times · something plays at a set time

    After the song = plays right after the song that is on air at that time (smooth, like a soft fixed time). Exactly on time = fades the song out at the minute and starts it. A playlist block always plays from the start (e.g. the Top 10). Time *:00 = every hour (e.g. the top-of-the-hour sweeper).
