Mixing · how one song goes into the next

At the old song's MIX point the new song starts at its full volume, and at the same moment the old song starts fading out underneath it – you still hear the end of the old one in the first seconds of the new one (like CLOSE). Cue points per song: Library page.

"Play now" in the panel/DJ decks is always a quick cut (0.25 s).

TV · paired songs

A song that has a clip with the same name in /clips: the radio plays the audio file, the TV plays the clip with its own sound. This sets how loud that clip sound is on TV compared to the radio.

Tickers · the scrolling bar at the bottom of the TV

A ticker reads an RSS feed (e.g. nu.nl/rss) or a Hype Live ticker link (hyperadio.nl/?hype_ticker=traffic …) every 10 minutes. Show it with Planning → ON SCREEN (e.g. "At a time *:20 → ▤ Ticker: TRAFFIC, 60 s"), or tick "always on the Hype Screen". A planned ticker goes over the always-on one; the bar gives way to the Now Playing box.
