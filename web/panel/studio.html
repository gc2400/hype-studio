Studio devices (this laptop)
Guests will hear themselves. Choose the input where the Mixcast's TRRS (mix-minus) comes in – e.g. the laptop's headset/mic jack with a TRRS cable. The Mixcast sends everything there except what comes in on that same TRRS fader, so guests hear you and the music, not themselves. (Guests hear each other directly on their page – never themselves.)
How to cable the Mixcast

USB laptop ↔ Mixcast: the music comes in on the USB fader, and the Mixcast's main mix goes back to the laptop (Studio mix → air).
TRRS cable laptop headphone/mic jack ↔ Mixcast TRRS: guests come in on the TRRS fader; the Mixcast sends a mix-minus (everything except the guests) back, so guests hear you and the music but not themselves.
Audio-only guest by phone: pair the phone with the Mixcast Bluetooth and call – the Mixcast does the mix-minus.
